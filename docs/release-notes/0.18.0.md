Nashville Numbers follows the song's harmony all the way through, riffs and solos too, and shows the next chord.

**Download `Re-ARMed-Mods-0.18.0.zip`**, open it and move **Re-ARMed Mods.app** to Applications, then click **Update Mods**. Quit the game first. The [README](https://github.com/glebb/rearmed-mods#install) has the steps for a first install.

## New

- **The song's chords throughout.** In 0.17.0 a numeral showed only where the part you play has a chord shape, so riffs, single-note lines and solos showed nothing. Now it shows the song's harmony bar by bar, the way a Nashville chart has it, whatever your part plays. It is worked out from all of the song's parts as it loads, with the bass part for the roots.
- **The next chord.** Under the current numeral, `next: V` says where the song goes next, as players read a chart a bar ahead.
- **Kept simple, like a chart.** A chord a bar, or two where it clearly changes in the middle. A riff counts as the chord it sits on rather than a chord for every note, unless it really moves between power chords (Smoke on the Water: the riff is `1m`, the verse `1 | 1 | 1 ♭7 | 1`, the chorus `4 | ♭2 | 1`). A bass walking down under a chord shows as a slash, `1/7`.
- **The old way is a setting.** **Follow** in the app (`NashvilleChords` in `RSMods.ini`): *the song* (the new default) or *the part played*, which shows that part's chords while they ring, as 0.17.0 did.

## How right it is

On the songs on disc, for every guitar part's chords, the harmony worked out from the song's other parts names the right root for 77% of them, and for 85% if a change half a bar early or late counts. Most of the rest is a walking or pedal bass taken for the root. The chord changes every 1.4 bars on average. The key is guessed as in 0.17.0, and **Q** still gives a song another key.

## Not yet tried

The harmony was seen in songs in the Mac edition by one player. Nashville Numbers hasn't been run in Learn & Play. The README says for each mod what has been checked.

## What you need

- An Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 13 or later for the app, and **RS Re-ARMed 1.5.1 or later**.
- **Nashville Numbers, Seven-String Guitar and Plain Packages need RS Re-ARMed 1.12.0 in Learn & Play and 1.13.0 on the Mac.** On the Mac, use RS Re-ARMed 1.15.1 or later with Seven-String Guitar: before it the game could stop in the middle of a song.
- **Pitch Shifter in Learn & Play needs the RS Re-ARMed after 1.17.1.** With an older one it stays off there. The Mac edition has what it needs.
- **Show Current Accuracy in Learn & Play's Score Attack needs RS Re-ARMed 1.14.0**, and so does Song Stems for a song another mod serves.
- **Song Stems needs RS Re-ARMed 1.12.0 and macOS 15 or later.**
- **Show Current Note, Guitar Speak, Show Current Accuracy, the grey-note reset, Prevent Buggy Tones and Custom Song List Titles need RS Re-ARMed 1.11.0.**
- **The colors need RS Re-ARMed 1.9.0**, and the note and highway colors need its Metal renderer. Sunlight needs the Metal renderer.
- Your Own Plug-in needs an Audio Unit effect of your own, such as Helix Native; plug-ins that are VST only don't work. Set Up Helix Native needs macOS's command line tools.
