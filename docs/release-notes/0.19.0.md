Pitch Shifter sets itself for each song from the song's tuning, and meets songs off concert pitch. On the Mac, Show Current Accuracy needs the RS Re-ARMed after 1.18.1 from this version on.

**Download `Re-ARMed-Mods-0.19.0.zip`**, open it and move **Re-ARMed Mods.app** to Applications, then click **Update Mods**. Quit the game first. If you use Your Own Plug-in, click **Update Tone Host** in its settings too. The [README](https://github.com/glebb/rearmed-mods#install) has the steps for a first install.

## New

- **Pitch Shifter follows the song.** Turn on **Set both for each song from its tuning** (`PitchShifterAuto`) and say once how your guitar is tuned (`PitchShifterGuitarTuning`: 0 is a guitar in E, -1 one in E flat). A song in E flat then gets `-1` on a guitar in E by itself, as its preview plays in the song list, so the tuner before the song already hears the shifted guitar. The keys still change the shift for the song in play. The idea is from [RocksmithAudioBridge](https://github.com/Cheesewizard/RocksmithAudioBridge)'s Drop Pedal.
- **The arrangement you pick counts.** Where a song's lead and rhythm are tuned apart, the shift goes by the lead in the song list and by the arrangement you picked from the moment the tuner opens.
- **Drop and open tunings** count as the tuning most of their strings are in (Eb Drop Db as E flat, DADGAD as E): the other strings still need retuning. A bass counts by its four strings.
- **Songs off concert pitch.** `PitchShifterA4` is the A your guitar sounds at in place of 440 Hz: 428 for a song in E Standard at A428. Control with the down or up key moves it a hertz while you play, and a key without Control goes back to the nearest semitone. With the automatic shift on, it is set from the song too (Castles Made of Sand, 45 cents down, gets A429).

## Fixed

- **Show Current Accuracy on the Mac.** It showed in some runs and not in others, and the grey-note reset of looping and rewinding went with it: the way the mod reached the game's note counts didn't hold from one run to the next. It now reaches them as the game's own code does. **That needs the RS Re-ARMed after 1.18.1**: with 1.18.1 or older nothing shows on the Mac, and the log says why. Learn & Play is as before.
- **Your Own Plug-in: the game's guitar never sounds alongside the host.** The game's guitar is held silent in the same moment the game, the mixer or a volume key sets it, not a frame later. Turning the mod off tells the host to quit at once.

## Not yet tried

The automatic shift was run in both editions over song lists, tuners and songs, and the values it chose were read from the log; it hasn't been played with a guitar. Show Current Accuracy was seen in Learn a Song on the Mac with a game built with the new names; Score Attack and the grey-note reset weren't run with it. The README says for each mod what has been checked.

## What you need

- An Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 13 or later for the app, and **RS Re-ARMed 1.5.1 or later**.
- **Show Current Accuracy and the grey-note reset need the RS Re-ARMed after 1.18.1 on the Mac**, and RS Re-ARMed 1.11.0 in Learn & Play (1.14.0 for the accuracy in its Score Attack).
- **Pitch Shifter in Learn & Play needs the RS Re-ARMed after 1.17.1.** With an older one it stays off there. The Mac edition has what it needs. Following the arrangement you picked needs RS Re-ARMed 1.13.0 on the Mac and 1.12.0 in Learn & Play.
- **Nashville Numbers, Seven-String Guitar and Plain Packages need RS Re-ARMed 1.12.0 in Learn & Play and 1.13.0 on the Mac.** On the Mac, use RS Re-ARMed 1.15.1 or later with Seven-String Guitar: before it the game could stop in the middle of a song.
- **Song Stems needs RS Re-ARMed 1.12.0 and macOS 15 or later**, and 1.14.0 for a song another mod serves.
- **Show Current Note, Guitar Speak, Prevent Buggy Tones and Custom Song List Titles need RS Re-ARMed 1.11.0.**
- **The colors need RS Re-ARMed 1.9.0**, and the note and highway colors need its Metal renderer. Sunlight needs the Metal renderer.
- Your Own Plug-in needs an Audio Unit effect of your own, such as Helix Native; plug-ins that are VST only don't work. Set Up Helix Native needs macOS's command line tools.
