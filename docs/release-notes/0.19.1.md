A fix for Control Game Volume in Learn & Play: the song stems key no longer changes the tone.

**Download `Re-ARMed-Mods-0.19.1.zip`**, open it and move **Re-ARMed Mods.app** to Applications, then click **Update Mods**. Quit the game first. The [README](https://github.com/glebb/rearmed-mods#install) has the steps for a first install.

## Fixed

- **The volume keys don't reach the game.** The mods saw every key and the game got it too. Learn & Play's game switches tones on 1 to 4, so in a song with stems the song stems key (4) changed the tone as well as the stems' level: the amp jumped and the tone was heard changing. The keys Control Game Volume acts on are now its own: the volume keys, the song stems key while the song has stems, the mixer, mute and select keys. In a song without stems 4 is the game's as before. The Mac game has no such use of the key.
- Nothing else is different from 0.19.0. What 0.19.0 brought, Pitch Shifter following each song's tuning among it, is in [its notes](https://github.com/glebb/rearmed-mods/releases/tag/v0.19.0).

## Not yet tried

The fix was tried in a song with stems in Learn & Play (4 changes the level and the tone stays) and on the Mac (the keys work as before). What 0.19.0's notes say hasn't been tried still hasn't.

## What you need

As for 0.19.0: an Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 13 or later for the app, and **RS Re-ARMed 1.5.1 or later**; what each mod needs beyond that is in [0.19.0's notes](https://github.com/glebb/rearmed-mods/releases/tag/v0.19.0) and the README.
