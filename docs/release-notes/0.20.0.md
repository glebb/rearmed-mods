Seven-String Guitar becomes extended range: RS Extended Range 1.0's charts load as they come, with 8-string guitars, 5- and 6-string basses and local co-op. **Needs RS Re-ARMed 1.21.0**, released together with this.

**Download `Re-ARMed-Mods-0.20.0.zip`**, open it and move **Re-ARMed Mods.app** to Applications, then click **Update Mods**. Quit the game first. Update RS Re-ARMed to 1.21.0 too. The [README](https://github.com/glebb/rearmed-mods#install) has the steps for a first install.

## New

- **RS Extended Range's own charts.** Packages named `*_rsxr_p.psarc` (DotCharter exports them) load in both editions as they are, and Plain Packages switches itself on for them. The song lists, the key finder, Extended Range, Tone Host and Song Stems read them too, also when Song Bridge serves them to the other edition.
- **8-string guitars**, with a pink 8th string and an 8-peg headstock.
- **5- and 6-string basses**: the strings, scoring, the tuner, the tuning names (B STANDARD 5STR), the neck and a grey low B, and their own headstocks.
- **Local co-op** builds each player's own instrument.
- **Settings in the app's 7-String page**: the string spacing of each instrument, hiding the added strings' lines (`string7_line`), the 7/8-row chord diagrams (`diagrams7`), the low-range warning (`lowrange`) and the 8th string's color.
- **RS Extended Range's fixes from its betas 4 to 6**: chord frames over every string, barre markers over the low string, the fret range glow and the camera over all strings, the highway floor placed again if the strings weren't ready, the tuner's outer peg, deeper low tunings heard, more tuning names.
- **Nashville Numbers** reads the 7th and 8th strings' notes and chords (an 8-string chart had no chords before).

## Fixed

- **A 5- or 6-string bass in Learn & Play** read a stray pointer where two of the mod's hooks share one place in the game.
- **The 8th string's headstock color** in Learn & Play, **8-string chord frames** with inverted strings, **`string7_line = 0`** dropping the added strings from note detection, and **the tuner** counting 8 strings on a 7-string guitar.
- **Pitch Shifter** judges a bass whose tuning matches none of the song's arrangements as a bass, not a guitar.

## Not yet tried

The 7- and 8-string charts and two 5-string basses were played in both editions by the new game tests (a test input in place of a guitar), and the 7- and 8-string Leads on the Mac by hand. Not yet: a six-string bass (no chart was at hand), local co-op with two inputs, and the spacing keys' look. The README says for each mod what has been checked.

## What you need

- An Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 13 or later for the app, and **RS Re-ARMed 1.5.1 or later**.
- **The extended-range parts need RS Re-ARMed 1.21.0**: with 1.20.0 or older they stay off and the log says which. 1.21.0 moved the added strings' lines into a group of their own, so rearmed-mods 0.19.1 and older lose those lines on it: update both together.
- What each other mod needs is in [0.19.0's notes](https://github.com/glebb/rearmed-mods/releases/tag/v0.19.0) and the README.
