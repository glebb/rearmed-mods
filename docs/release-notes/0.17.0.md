Nashville Numbers shows the chord you're playing as a numeral of the song's key.

**Download `Re-ARMed-Mods-0.17.0.zip`**, open it and move **Re-ARMed Mods.app** to Applications, then click **Update Mods**. Quit the game first. The [README](https://github.com/glebb/rearmed-mods#install) has the steps for a first install.

## New

- **Nashville Numbers.** In a song, the chord being played as a numeral of the song's key, right of the middle with the key under it: as Roman numerals (`IV`, `vi`, `♭VII`, `V7`, `I/3`) or as Nashville numbers (`4`, `6m`, `♭7`, `57`, `1/3`). Turn it on in the app's **Screen** page; its settings choose the style, whether a minor key is counted from its own tonic (Am is `1m`) or from its relative major (`6m`), and how much of a chord to show.
- **The song's key is a guess.** Rocksmith songs don't say their key, so the mod guesses it from the notes of all of a song's arrangements as the song loads. On the songs on disc whose key is known it has the tonic right for 44 of 50, and for 3 more the right one is its second guess: songs built on a ♭VII (I–♭VII–IV, as Sweet Home Alabama) can come out a fourth up. The **Q** key gives the song its next guess and keeps it for that song; **Control + Q** goes back to the guess. Keys are at the chart's pitch, which isn't always the record's.
- **Simple or full chords.** By default a chord is shown as a Nashville chart writes it, what it does in the key rather than how it's voiced: a power chord is just its number, sus2, add9, 6 and maj7 are left off, a 9 shows as its 7, sus4 as `sus`. **Full** shows what the chord's notes say. The chords are named from their notes, since the charts' own names are often missing.

## Fixed

- Text the mods draw with a flat sign or another character Helvetica Bold lacks now sits as the rest of the text does; before, macOS drew it from a font that spaced it well apart.

## Not yet tried

Nashville Numbers was seen in songs in the Mac edition by one player; it hasn't been run in Learn & Play, whose song files were only checked by the tests. The key guesses were measured offline against the songs on disc. The README says for each mod what has been checked.

## What you need

- An Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 13 or later for the app, and **RS Re-ARMed 1.5.1 or later**.
- **Nashville Numbers, Seven-String Guitar and Plain Packages need RS Re-ARMed 1.12.0 in Learn & Play and 1.13.0 on the Mac.** On the Mac, use RS Re-ARMed 1.15.1 or later with Seven-String Guitar: before it the game could stop in the middle of a song.
- **Pitch Shifter in Learn & Play needs the RS Re-ARMed after 1.17.1.** With an older one it stays off there. The Mac edition has what it needs.
- **Show Current Accuracy in Learn & Play's Score Attack needs RS Re-ARMed 1.14.0**, and so does Song Stems for a song another mod serves.
- **Song Stems needs RS Re-ARMed 1.12.0 and macOS 15 or later.**
- **Show Current Note, Guitar Speak, Show Current Accuracy, the grey-note reset, Prevent Buggy Tones and Custom Song List Titles need RS Re-ARMed 1.11.0.**
- **The colors need RS Re-ARMed 1.9.0**, and the note and highway colors need its Metal renderer. Sunlight needs the Metal renderer.
- Your Own Plug-in needs an Audio Unit effect of your own, such as Helix Native; plug-ins that are VST only don't work. Set Up Helix Native needs macOS's command line tools.
